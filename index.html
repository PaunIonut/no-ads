Fewer interruptions. Simple control.

No Ads — Video Cleaner is an independent Chrome extension published by Paun Ionut. It reduces many ads and promotional interruptions on compatible YouTube pages. Results may vary when the service changes.

Local processing

Included rules and page cleanup run in the browser. There is no first-party analytics or telemetry.

Explicit control

SponsorBlock and aggressive mode are disabled by default. SponsorBlock contacts its third-party service only after the user enables it.

Accurate claims

No Ads — Video Cleaner does not guarantee removal of every ad and is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, or SponsorBlock.
